One small delivery, five steps

  1. Brief: paste the promised deliverables. Use non-confidential material for a first trial.
  2. Checklist: create items manually or request Gemma suggestions. Compare every item against the saved brief: quantities, formats, exclusions and omissions. Confirm only after correcting it.
  3. Files: add the actual delivery files. Inspect properties and duplicate warnings. A failed replacement keeps the existing original; a successful change makes affected reviews stale.
  4. Review: choose evidence, preview checks, inspect actual content and record your assessment. “Satisfied” does not erase a failed technical check.
  5. Handoff: inspect the client preview, including unresolved work and public notes. Save a snapshot and download HTML, Markdown or JSON. Send the original files alongside it.

Changing the source, checklist or packet requires fresh review. Earlier records remain unchanged. Private notes stay out of the client export; requirement titles and designated client notes are shared.

Three kinds of evidence

What to trust—and what still needs you
Source What it establishes Your next check
Gemma suggestion A proposed requirement or file association Verify the exact source, omitted promises and whether content actually meets the requirement.
File inspection A narrow property such as pixel dimensions or duplicate bytes Inspect visual quality and meaning. Different bytes do not prove distinct designs.
Your assessment Your recorded judgment of the current packet Resolve gaps, explain exceptions and review again after changes.

A source quote proves provenance, not correctness. A missing suggestion does not mean the brief has no requirement. There is no calibrated model confidence score.

Where the desk stops

Supported files include PNG, JPEG, SVG metadata, readable PDF, text and Markdown within documented limits. It does not run websites, test links or forms, scan accessibility, deploy code, certify designs, accept payment, send files or establish client acceptance. Scanned PDFs and arbitrary archives are not supported.

In the frozen synthetic V4 evaluation, extraction covered 15 of 21 mandatory obligations. On separately supplied requirements, guards retained 19 correct file associations and removed two wrong-format associations from the same captured outputs. These small synthetic results are not real-world accuracy or evidence that semantic mistakes cannot occur.

The model runs locally after installation. Optional AI requires model/runtime downloads and adequate hardware; manual review remains available without it.
