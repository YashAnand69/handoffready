# Week 1 winners: category breadth research

Research date: 17 October 2026. The [official DEV announcement](https://dev.to/devteam/congrats-to-the-hacktoberfest-open-source-ai-challenge-touch-grass-winners-152c), published 16 October, confirms the categories below. This document complements the separate deep reads of Overall/Paper Trail, Gemma/Grow a Song, ElevenLabs/Outside and Tinker. All implementation, test, latency and user-trial evidence below is **reported by the winning authors**; we have not run their apps, reproduced their experiments or independently audited repositories. Winning status does not establish why judges chose them. We have no judge scorecards or feedback on Soundwalk Atlas.

## Cross-entry lessons for HandoffReady

1. **Do not infer a single winning recipe.** Some winners have field users; some openly lack trials. Some are local-only, others hosted. The consistent opportunity is specific, inspectable evidence around a clear user outcome, not checking every technology box.
2. **A useful artifact is memorable.** A calendar invitation, printable card, photography plan or branch-changing photo has a tangible purpose. HandoffReady should demonstrate a client record a freelancer can explain and use.
3. **Keep AI and coded facts distinct.** This appears repeatedly in the articles. For HandoffReady, extraction/matching is fallible; hashes, file checks, review currency and human disposition must remain separate and visibly truthful.
4. **Show one failure, its diagnosis and its consequence.** Good examples include global-versus-local duration, nonexistent lens settings, secret scrubbing that removes telemetry and hemisphere-only season rules. We must use our own actual evidence, never transplant a competitor's story.
5. **Live-model evaluation differs from app tests.** A large unit-test total can coexist with missing requirements, misleading prose or a poor interaction. Publish exact evaluation denominators and omissions, using a fresh set if changing the model pipeline.
6. **Honest AI assistance is compatible with winning.** Several winning authors explicitly credit coding agents and explain the human decisions. Do not hide assistance or manufacture personal experience to sound “human.”
7. **Our constraints remain real.** No invented freelancer feedback, synthetic results portrayed as customer outcomes, broad privacy claims without boundaries or promised functionality unsupported by the current build.

These are analytical lessons from published entries, **not conclusions about private judging decisions** and not a guarantee of Week 2 selection.
