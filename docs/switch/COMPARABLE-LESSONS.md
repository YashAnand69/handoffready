The focused story is: a designer sends a delivery packet, the assistant finds a sourced problem and asks about an ambiguity, the designer supplies context/correction, and the lead reviews the exact new version before deliberately sharing its record.

Our distinguishing claim must be the demonstrated brief-to-delivery clarification loop, not agent count, test count or an automated approval label. A count of three different file hashes does not prove three different designs. A passed dimension check does not establish visual suitability. A local test does not establish remote collaboration.

For the genuine trial, record whether the tester can find their next action, whether the question changes an actual decision, what the assistant gets wrong, lead clicks/tab switches, total active effort and the returned record's usefulness. Do not turn one successful example into a universal speed or accuracy claim. Fix observed friction before adding optional features.
