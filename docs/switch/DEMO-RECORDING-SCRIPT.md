# Your recording guide — HandoffReady Delivery Room

Draft, 6 November 2026. Record after the current bug-fix candidate passes checks. This is a recording plan, not a claim that footage exists. Publication and submission are held for Yash's video and review.

## The message

“A team needs more than a folder of files. It needs to connect the agreed work, the latest evidence and an accountable review.”

Show Switch in the first 15 seconds. Show a correction changing the outcome. Finish with the actual returned record. Aim for about three minutes; check the submission form for any stated video limit before recording.

## Before pressing record

Use synthetic material only. Close private chats, notifications and credential forms. Keep Switch Console, the configured provider and the correct local desk running. Use a dedicated recording fixture/job; preserve the completed owner rehearsal. Confirm its exact room and agent instructions before uploading. Do not assume that creating a new project rebinds an existing room.

Record at a readable desktop size with the pointer visible. Use normal motion settings or reduced motion according to your preference; do not speed through confirmations. Do not fill an assessment merely because the script says to: inspect the evidence and choose your actual judgment. Keep an unedited source recording; label any cuts that remove waits.

Do not present an earlier legacy return as a newly authorized current record. New conversation questions/replies require fresh authorization even after their hold clears.

## On-screen labels

- Opening: “Synthetic owner-operated demonstration · AI-assisted build.”
- Any shortened wait: “Wait shortened; original recording retained.”
- Local review: “Human judgment — saved by Yash.”
- Return: “Operator-confirmed · exact downloaded bytes + destination check.”
- Closing: “Local desk + Switch + Discord + configured agent provider. Shared material may be processed by chat/AI providers.”

For a rehearsal using the already completed evidence, label it “Walkthrough of recorded owner rehearsal.” Do not enact missing actions and describe them as the original run. An authored demo is legitimate evidence of behavior when its operation and inputs are disclosed.
