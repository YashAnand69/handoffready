# Winner-informed submission revision — 18 October 2026

## Scope and evidence

This editorial review covers the **17 entries named in the official Week 1 winner announcement**: one overall, six featured-category and ten partner-category winners. It builds on the 17 October [four deep reads](WEEK1-POSTMORTEM-AND-WEEK2-RESPONSE.md) and [thirteen-entry comparison](WEEK1-WINNERS-BREADTH.md). One follow-up web excerpt for Should I Go Out failed; its earlier read remains the source of that row. This is an article analysis, not execution of competitors' apps or an independent audit of their reported measurements.

Sources: [official Week 1 announcement](https://dev.to/devteam/congrats-to-the-hacktoberfest-open-source-ai-challenge-touch-grass-winners-152c) and [current Week 2 prompt](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12). A first user is a bonus. We do not have judge scorecards, so patterns below are editorial inferences, not established reasons for winning. This covers Week 1, not every DEV contest ever run.

## Revision decisions

- Preserve the concrete flawed-folder opening and connect it immediately to the client record.
- Add four bounded use cases: brand exports, campaign images, written guides and revised delivery. Each separates measured facts from the person's judgment.
- Move human contribution near the beginning. Attribute audience/constraints/workflow direction to Yash and implementation/evaluation/documentation to AI agents. Product reviewer responsibility is a separate role.
- Identify a prospective buyer and service hypothesis. Do not fabricate income, saved hours, buyer interest or a personal client incident.
- Keep the real failure-and-correction sequence, source links and current 18 October checks. Remove the redundant older test-count paragraph; retain the older recording date.
- Preserve required headings, challenge link, tags, Gemma category, cover, public URL and AI disclosure. Update the existing entry only.
- Do not copy competitors' phrasing, personal experiences, testimonials, data or metrics into the submission.
