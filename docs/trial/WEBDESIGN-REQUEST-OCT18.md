## Web/design freelancers: would this client delivery record help your handoff, or add paperwork?

I’m looking for 1–3 web/design freelancers to give candid feedback on HandoffReady, a free, open-source prototype for checking outgoing files against a brief. This is voluntary and unpaid; criticism is welcome.

The narrow workflow is: confirm the agreed checklist, inspect supporting files, record what is ready or still needs work, then export a client-facing record. Local Gemma proposes requirements/file matches; people review them. It doesn’t run a website or judge design quality.

You can inspect the recorded synthetic example without installing anything:
https://yashanand69.github.io/handoffready/explore/
Example client record:
https://yashanand69.github.io/handoffready/records/after.html

These are saved examples, not the live application. Three questions:
- How do you currently check and explain a delivery to a client?
- Is it clear what remains unresolved in the example record? What is confusing or missing?
- Would you actually use something like this on your next project, or does your current process already solve it better?

If you’re willing to try the full app, reply here. It runs locally; the validated setup is Apple Silicon macOS, and manual review works without downloading the optional model. Source/setup: https://github.com/YashAnand69/handoffready

Please don’t share client files or confidential details. I’ll ask separately before quoting any feedback in the project write-up. I’m developing this for Hacktoberfest Week 2, with AI agents assisting implementation/testing and this request; my role is product direction. A real freelancer trial has not happened yet.
