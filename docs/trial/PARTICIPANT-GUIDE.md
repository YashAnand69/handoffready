# Try HandoffReady: a 20-minute delivery review

HandoffReady is a prototype for web/design freelancers checking outgoing files against an agreed brief. We'd like your honest feedback—not a positive review. We are testing the tool, not you. You can stop at any time.

## Before you start

Try it together on Yash's Mac, or install the project on your own supported computer using its README. A `127.0.0.1` link only works on the computer running the app; it is not a shareable hosted application. Model installation is optional for a manual trial. A hosted recording is a demonstration, not the live app.

Bring a small, non-confidential brief and a few supported files you have permission to use. Remove client names, credentials and personal information. Alternatively use the clearly labelled synthetic sample. No recording is necessary. Permission to try the tool does not grant permission to publish your comments or identity.

## Before opening it — 2 minutes

Explain how you normally check a delivery before sending it. What was difficult about the last handoff you completed? Avoid sharing confidential details.

## Try these tasks — 12 minutes

Please say what you expect and what feels unclear. Work in your usual way; ask for help when needed.

1. Start a small delivery review and capture what was promised.
2. Make a checklist you trust. If using AI, compare its suggestions with the original brief and correct anything missing or wrong.
3. Add or inspect the delivery files. Decide which files support each requirement and whether they are sufficient.
4. Prepare a record you would feel comfortable sending to a client. Explain what it does—and does not—tell the client.
5. Change one test file or requirement. Work out what needs attention before sending a new record.

The facilitator may stop a task after three minutes of being stuck. That is useful feedback, not a failure on your part. The exported record does not include your original files.

## Tell us what happened — 6 minutes

- What, if anything, helped you that your normal process does not?
- Where did you hesitate, need help or distrust the result?
- What did you expect the tool to check that it did not?
- Would you choose it for your next actual delivery? Why or why not?
- What would need to change before you used it regularly?
- Would you pay for this as it stands? “No” is useful. If yes, what alternative or cost are you comparing it with? We are not taking payment.

You may approve a specific anonymized quote separately after seeing its exact wording and destination. Otherwise feedback stays private. Share observations, not client files.
